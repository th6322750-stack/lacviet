# Lạc Việt Media Agency — Approved Direction

## Visual identity

- Primary mood: premium, trustworthy, corporate, Vietnamese-inspired.
- Core palette: near-black / charcoal backgrounds with restrained metallic-gold accents.
- Brand motif: Lạc Việt bird/phoenix emblem as the primary visual signature.
- Avoid: neon cyberpunk, gamer styling, aggressive red/black underground styling, generic SaaS blue UI.
- Typography direction: premium display serif for selected major headlines + clean sans-serif for body/UI.
- Surfaces: dark matte texture, subtle gold linework, elegant borders, restrained glow only where needed.

## Public service positioning

1. Multi-platform advertising management and optimization.
2. Official appeal/support for legitimate owners of restricted or disabled Facebook/TikTok advertising accounts.
3. Advertising account/business infrastructure solutions where lawful and platform-compliant.
4. AI/software/digital products only where lawful, authorized, and redistributable.
5. MMO consulting and training.

## Landing-page structure

1. Header / brand / primary CTA
2. Hero — premium brand promise + key CTA
3. Supported advertising platforms
4. Service grid
5. Why choose Lạc Việt
6. Trust / proof section (real metrics only; no fabricated claims)
7. Process / how it works
8. FAQ / compliance-safe explanations
9. Final CTA
10. Footer / verified contact channels

## Copy guardrails

- Do not advertise bypassing platform security or unauthorized account access.
- Do not claim guaranteed account recovery.
- Do not fabricate customer counts, success rates, revenue, ROAS, or case studies.
- Do not advertise unauthorized/shared/stolen subscriptions or credentials.
- Recovery-related copy must be framed as legitimate appeal/support for the rightful account owner.
